🔑 Apps & credentials you've issued

A credential is a key you give to a program — a Discord bot, a bridge, a script — so it can read or write in your pod without you present.

⚠︎ A credential acts as you. It carries your identity and can do whatever you can do in your pod — today it can't be limited to just one app's data. Your real safety controls are: share as little as possible with each thing (least-privilege), name credentials clearly, and revoke the moment you're done. Revoking is instant.
